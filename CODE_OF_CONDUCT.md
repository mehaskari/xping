# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the xping community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Standards

**Positive:** welcoming language, respectful viewpoints, constructive criticism, empathy.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information.

## Enforcement

Report to **iorganamis@gmail.com**. Violations result in:
Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
